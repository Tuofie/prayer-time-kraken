# Prayer Time for Kraken

A free NZXT Kraken LCD screen showing:
- the **next prayer** with a live countdown and a progress ring (Jumu'ah on Fridays)
- your **CPU, GPU and liquid temperatures**
- all five prayer times and the **Hijri date**

It comes in **English and Arabic**, in three styles (Night & gold, Rose, Mint), and works for any city with 12 calculation methods.

## Set it up (2 minutes)
1. Open the **setup page**: https://tuofie.github.io/prayer-time-kraken/setup.html
2. Pick your city, method, language and style, then press **Copy link**.
3. In **NZXT CAM**, go to **Lighting → LCD Display → Web Integration → Custom Web Integration**, paste your link and save.

Works with Kraken Elite and Kraken (Z-series) coolers that have a screen.

If it stops drawing, set **CAM → Settings → General → Graphics API** to DirectX 12.
